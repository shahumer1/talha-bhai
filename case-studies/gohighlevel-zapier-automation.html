Technical FAQ

About this lead management system

How does the lead capture workflow work?

Leads are captured through webhooks in Zapier. The workflow processes the data, applies conditional logic, looks up records in Google Sheets, and routes the lead to the correct GoHighLevel opportunity pipeline with appropriate stage and status.

What follow-up sequences are automated?

The system sends immediate confirmation emails and SMS, 48-hour, 24-hour, and 1-hour appointment reminders, plus internal team notifications when action is required.

How are team handoffs handled?

The handoff workflow detects customer replies requiring human assistance. Matching conversations are routed to a team member with an internal email notification, while non-matching replies exit the branch.

What custom logic is used in Zapier?

The workflows use paths, filters, storage, loops, digest, custom Python steps, and Google Sheets lookups to process data before sending it to GoHighLevel via LeadConnector API.

How are errors handled?

The workflows include explicit success and error branches. This prevents silent failures and ensures issues are surfaced for troubleshooting.
