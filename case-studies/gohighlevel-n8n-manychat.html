Case Study FAQ

Technical questions

What tools were used in this automation?

The implementation used GoHighLevel, n8n, ManyChat, Instagram messaging, AI qualification logic and webhook integrations.

Why were GoHighLevel and n8n both used?

GoHighLevel handled CRM state, tags, contact fields, message buffering and workflow controls. n8n provided webhook-driven orchestration and connected structured conversation state with the AI reasoning layer.

What does the 1,132 figure represent?

It represents verified workflow enrollments visible in the supplied GoHighLevel implementation evidence. It does not represent customers, conversions or revenue.

How were fragmented Instagram messages handled?

Message-buffer workflows collected rapid or fragmented replies before another AI processing cycle, helping the reasoning layer work with more complete conversation context.

How did the system control when AI should stop?

Contact fields, tags, branch conditions and AI ON/OFF state were used to control whether automation remained active, stopped or routed a qualified prospect forward.
