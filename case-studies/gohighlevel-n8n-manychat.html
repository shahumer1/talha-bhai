Business Objective

Turn Instagram engagement into a controlled qualification flow

The objective was to move inbound Instagram engagement into an automated qualification process instead of relying on immediate manual outreach for every prospect.

The system engages prospects, evaluates intent, maintains qualification state inside the CRM, and provides a structured path for qualified prospects to reach the team.

Core Outcome

Automated first-touch with controlled human handoff

The automation handles early conversation and qualification while keeping the CRM state available for the team when human involvement is required.

Technical FAQ

About this automation architecture

How does the Instagram AI lead qualification system work?

Instagram engagement enters through ManyChat. GoHighLevel maintains CRM and conversation state, while n8n sends structured context into the AI qualification logic. The returned action is then used to continue, stop or route the prospect forward.

Why are GoHighLevel and n8n both used?

GoHighLevel acts as the CRM and workflow-state layer for tags, fields, buffers and contact status. n8n provides webhook-driven orchestration between that structured state and the AI reasoning layer.

How are multiple short Instagram messages handled?

Message-buffer workflows collect rapid or fragmented replies and update the correct state before the next AI processing cycle. This allows the reasoning layer to work with more complete conversation context.

What role does ManyChat play in the system?

ManyChat provides the Instagram DM entry and trigger layer. It starts the messaging flow before the qualification state is managed across GoHighLevel, n8n and the AI layer.

How does the automation decide when to stop AI or hand off a lead?

Contact fields, tags, branch conditions and AI ON/OFF state are used to control whether automation continues, stops or routes a qualified prospect forward.
