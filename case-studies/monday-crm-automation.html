Business Objective

Multi-pipeline deal tracking with stage-triggered follow-up

The real estate business needed a centralized CRM to manage investors, sellers, service providers and brokers across separate pipelines while automating follow-up communication and task coordination.

The system was designed to move deals through stages, trigger automated emails and notifications, and maintain visibility of every opportunity in one workspace.

Core Outcome

Structured pipeline with automated stage-based outreach

Deals progress through defined stages with automated email sequences, internal notifications, and nurture workflows that reduce manual follow-up effort.

Technical FAQ

About this Monday.com CRM system

What pipelines were configured in Monday.com?

Four opportunity pipelines were configured: Investors, Sellers/Owners, Service Providers, and Brokers/Agents. Each pipeline has its own stage progression and deal tracking fields.

How are automations triggered?

Stage and status changes trigger automations. For example, when a deal moves to a new stage, an email is sent, and a timed delay with a conditional check follows before the next action.

What email integration was used?

Gmail email actions are connected inside Monday.com workflows. This allows automated emails to be sent to contacts or team members based on stage changes and conditional logic.

How is investor nurture handled?

A separate active nurture workflow waits 30 days, re-checks status, then sends an email when the condition is met, ensuring long-term follow-up without manual intervention.

What supporting boards exist?

Supporting boards include Organizations, Lists, Tasks, Contacts, Relationships, and Deals. These provide reference data and structured relationships around the opportunity pipelines.
