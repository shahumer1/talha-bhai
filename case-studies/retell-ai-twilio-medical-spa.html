Technical FAQ

About this AI voice receptionist

How does the AI receptionist book appointments?

The inbound agent uses two callable functions: check_cal_availability to verify open slots and book_appointment to schedule. Both functions require a user-confirmed date and time before triggering, preventing premature bookings.

What's the difference between inbound and outbound agents?

The inbound agent handles incoming calls for service questions and bookings. The outbound agent handles follow-up calls with dynamic caller fields for identity confirmation, timing checks, and callback paths when the timing isn't suitable.

How is Twilio configured for this system?

Twilio is configured with an Elastic SIP trunk named for Retell integration. It handles origination (inbound SIP traffic toward Retell) and termination (outbound PSTN routing) with a US phone number assigned under the trunk.

What conversation tuning was applied?

Response behavior, interruption sensitivity, call-center background sound, noise removal, transcription mode (optimized for speed), keypad input detection with timeout, silence handling, and maximum call duration controls were all explicitly configured.

How are dates and times handled consistently?

Current-time context is passed through a dynamic variable and interpreted in US Eastern Time. Internal date/time rules specify an absolute RFC3339 datetime with offset for function calls, ensuring consistent interpretation.
