Business Objective

Share candidate value without exposing contact details

The recruitment business received resumes by email and needed to present qualified candidates to relevant companies while protecting candidate contact information.

If a company wanted to proceed, it contacted the recruiter to obtain full candidate details and complete the commercial process — keeping the recruiter in control of the relationship.

Companies receive sanitized resumes for review while the recruiter retains control of original candidate details and the commercial process.

Technical FAQ

About this resume anonymization workflow

How does the resume anonymization workflow work?

The workflow monitors incoming email, retrieves resume attachments, converts documents to text, uses AI Builder to extract and remove contact information, and generates a sanitized document for client distribution while preserving recruiter control.

What file formats are supported?

The workflow supports both PDF and Word document attachments. Files are normalized, processed, and rebuilt as sanitized PDF or Word documents.

How is candidate data protected?

Original data remains protected and recruiters retain control of the candidate relationship. Companies receive only sanitized resumes and must contact the recruiter to obtain full details.

How are duplicate applications handled?

The workflow includes duplicate checks using full name, email and phone comparisons against existing records before processing resume attachments.

How is invoice status tracked?

Invoice lifecycle states (draft, review, confirmed, sent, billed, paid, partially paid, overdue, void) are represented and synchronized across Excel and Dataverse.
