Technical FAQ

About this Airtable operations system

What does this Airtable system manage?

The system manages product lifecycle (concept through completion), task assignments with Slack notifications, lead tracking, follow-up scheduling, and opportunity progression for commercial activity.

How are Slack notifications triggered?

When a task is assigned to a team member, an Airtable automation finds the matching Slack user record and sends a direct message notification to that team member.

What views are available to the team?

The system includes grid, Kanban and calendar views so different team members can work in the format that best suits their role and workflow.

How is product lifecycle progression controlled?

Stage rules, previous-stage order and stage allowances control forward and backward progression. Automations update timing fields and preserve stage history.

Can this system be expanded?

Yes. The modular Airtable structure can be expanded with additional teams, products, workflows and commercial tracking as the business grows.
