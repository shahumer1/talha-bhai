Technical FAQ

About AI receptionists

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is a voice agent that answers inbound calls, handles customer inquiries, qualifies leads, books appointments, and updates your CRM — all through natural conversation.

What types of businesses can use an AI receptionist?

AI receptionists are used by medical spas, clinics, law firms, agencies, real estate, and any service business that receives inbound calls for appointments or inquiries.

How does the AI handle complex questions?

The AI is configured with conversation logic and business rules. It can answer FAQs, collect information, and seamlessly hand off to a human when a complex question arises.

Can the AI book appointments directly?

Yes. The AI can check real-time availability and book appointments directly using calendar APIs and scheduling functions.

What happens if a caller needs to speak to a human?

The AI detects when a caller needs human assistance and transfers the call with full conversation context so the team can follow up without repeating information.
